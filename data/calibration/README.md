# Calibration Data

Instrument calibration datasets for the Neutron Spectrometer and NIR Camera.

## NS Calibration
- Ground calibration measurements (Cf-252 source)
- Energy calibration coefficients
- Dead-time correction lookup tables

## NIR Camera Calibration
- Dark frame stacks at operating temperatures
- Flat-field frames
- Spectral calibration (wavelength-to-pixel mapping)
- Radiometric calibration coefficients

*Calibration data files will be committed during Phase 4 (instrument integration)*
